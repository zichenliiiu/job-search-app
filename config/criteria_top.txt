The user is a Product Strategy professional with 7 years of experience at McKinsey (as a
management consultant), Gem
(high-growth B2B SaaS, in a Business Operations role ), and Atlassian (in a Product Strategy role).
The user would like to pivot to Product Management.
Considering that the user has no direct Product Management experience, they are also open
to continue working in Strategy roles (including Business Operations) supporting Product or Engineering, or Strategy roles
that serves across functions of the company (Product, Engineering, and GTM). Chief of Staff roles
are relevant too.

A "top" job looks like the below, in this order of preference:
- Jobs with years of experience requirement that's within the users' years of experience (7 years)
- Product Management roles where the job description specifically says they do not require prior product management
experience / open to candidates with prior expereince outside of product management. For example, the requirement
section may read "Minimum 4 years of product management or adjacent product-building experience". If the requirement
is that the candidate must have prior product management experience, ie, other adjacent experience does not count
, or if the requirement is prior product management experience or engineering experience, then the job
should not be "top"
- Chief of Staff roles, where the functions they support includes Product, Engineering. For example,
Chief of Staff to the Chief Product Officer, Chief of Staff to the Chief Technology officer, or
Chief of Staff to the CEO. However, Chief of Staff roles that only support go-to-market (sometimes abbreviated as "GTM"),
or sub functions within go-to-market such as Sales, Customer Success, should not be "top"
- Pricing roles that fall under Product (as opposed to Finance or GTM) would be "top"
- Strategy roles (for example, "Strategy and Operations", "Strategy & Operations") that support
Product or Engineering specifically should be "top"

A job should NOT be "top" if any of the following apply:
- years of experience requirement is more than 20% above what the user has
- Product management roles that specifically require prior Product Management experience, and not open to
candidates with background similar to but not exactly product management
- Strategy roles that support GTM functions specifically, e.g., GTM Strategy and Operations
- Roles that are in the following functions (or support the following functions):
partnerships, corporate development, customer success, people function (e.g., HR), Finance
(sometimes labeled "Strategic Finance"), product operations ("Strategy and Operations" are exempted
from this)
- Roles that are specified to be in a region that the user cannot work from, ie, the role is
not in San Francisco Bay Area, or New York, not remote either.
